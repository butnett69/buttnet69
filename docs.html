Getting started with Buttnet 69

Buttnet Docs — v0.1.0

Getting started is as easy as sitting down. The network welcomes all wallets, regardless of size, provided they can sit.

Quick start

Contract address

1. Get TAO on Solana

$BUTT69 is priced in TAO, not SOL. You need TAO on Solana. Swap SOL or USDC for it on Jupiter or Raydium, and check the mint address before you confirm. Anything else calling itself TAO on Solana is not the one this pairs with.

2. Buy $BUTT69

It trades on a Raydium pool with locked liquidity, priced in TAO, and shows up on Jupiter and Dexscreener like any other token. Buy it from StonkFun's token page or straight through Jupiter.

3. Sit

Now you can be a validator. TAO arrives in your wallet when the platform runs a reward cycle. There is nothing to claim and nothing to stake. Max sit.

Market

Mechanism

Transfer tax3% on every transfer, in and out, on every venue. It is part of the token, not a pool setting.
Who collects itThe launch platform (StonkFun). Only its authority can move the tax.
Paid inTAO on Solana.
Split95% to holders, pro-rata by how much they are sitting on. The platform keeps 5% and spends it buying its own favourites.
CadencePlatform-run reward cycles. Historically about hourly. Not guaranteed.

Running a validator

Validators are the seat of the network. To become one, sit on $BUTT69. To stop being one, stand up, at which point you become a miner for exactly one transaction and are thanked for your service.
