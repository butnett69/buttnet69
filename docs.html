Getting started with Buttnet 69

Buttnet Docs — v0.1.0

Getting started is as easy as sitting down. The network welcomes all wallets, regardless of size, provided they can hold.

Quick start

Contract address

1. Get TAO on Solana

$BUTT69 is priced in TAO, not SOL. You need canonical TAO on Solana, mint . Swap SOL or USDC for it on Jupiter or Raydium, and check the mint address before you confirm; anything else calling itself TAO on Solana is not the one this pairs with.

2. Buy on the curve, or on Raydium after graduation

Until the curve fills (~34 TAO of buys) the token trades on StonkFun's LaunchLab page. After graduation it trades on a Raydium pool with locked liquidity, and shows up on Jupiter and Dexscreener like any other token.

3. Hold it in a self-custody wallet

That's the whole validator role. TAO arrives in your wallet when the platform runs a reward cycle. There is nothing to claim and nothing to stake. Tokens sitting on an exchange or in a pool don't count as eligible balances.

Running a validator

Validators are the seat of the network. To become one, hold $BUTT69. To stop being one, sell it, at which point you become a miner for exactly one transaction, and are thanked for your service.

Questions people ask

When do I get paid?

When the platform runs a reward cycle. Look for incoming TAO transfers in your wallet history. Amounts depend on how much $BUTT69 was transferred during the cycle and your share of eligible balances. Very small balances can round to nothing.

Where does the TAO come from?

From other people transferring $BUTT69. A transfer tax is a cost on transfers, not a return on holding. If trading slows, payouts slow. That is the mechanism working as designed, not a bug.

How do I check a payout is real?

Open the incoming transfer in your wallet history. Confirm the mint is the TAO mint shown in the footer, and match the sender against the platform's distribution records on StonkFun's token page. A "Received" label on its own proves nothing.

Is this affiliated with Buttensor, Bittensor, Herald or StonkFun?

No. It uses StonkFun's public launch model, copies $BUTT's mechanism with attribution, and borrows Herald's tagline out of context. Nobody's team is involved but ours.

Is there a treasury, a roadmap, a team allocation?

No treasury. No roadmap beyond the three lines on the home page. One dev wallet, published above.

Will the dev sell?

Maybe. The wallet is public, so you'll see it if it happens. Every transfer out of it pays the same 3% as yours.
